Finland

Elecz ranks Finnish contracts by estimated total cost. Commercial partnerships do not affect the order.

Where the data comes from

Contract data comes from the Finnish Energy Authority's Sähkönhinta.fi service and is updated automatically once or twice a day.

How offers are ranked

1. Eligibility. We include offers available nationally or at the postcode you give. Without a postcode, only national offers are compared, and the results say so.

2. Cost estimate. Each offer gets an estimated cost for the first 12 months of the contract, including energy price, standing charges and structured discounts.

3. Order. Offers are ranked from cheapest estimated cost, separately for spot-price and fixed-price contracts.

Spot prices and grid fees. The annual cost of spot-price contracts is estimated from a historical reference price. The actual cost depends on future spot prices and may differ from the estimate. Grid (distribution) fees are not included, because the grid company is set by your location and does not change when you switch electricity contract.

Assumptions

If you don't enter your own details, the comparison uses clearly stated defaults, such as a typical annual consumption for your housing type and a standard main fuse size. Your own details replace the defaults.

What is left out

  • Offers whose price can't yet be compared reliably, such as some time-of-use, seasonal or consumption-linked pricing. These are not shown in the top lists.
  • Restricted offers, such as those only for students or certain age groups, are not in the default lists.
  • New-customer offers are shown but labelled.

Comparisons may not cover every offer on the market. Always check the final price and terms with the supplier.

Commercial partnerships

Using the Elecz comparison is free. We fund it through affiliate commissions: when you sign up through a partner link, the supplier or an affiliate network (such as Adtraction) may pay Elecz a commission.

  • Partnerships do not affect the ranking. An offer does not move up because it pays a commission.
  • Offers that pay us nothing are included in comparisons too.
  • Partner links are marked on the comparison page.

Offer data and costs

Elecz uses offer data from Portale Offerte. Electricity suppliers are responsible for their own offer information. Elecz calculates its own annual estimates for the electricity sold by the supplier. Network charges, system charges and taxes fall outside this estimate.

The comparison uses the annual consumption entered, assumes 3 kW of contracted power and models a household switching supplier. Elecz uses offers with price terms that cover the 12-month comparison period and whose charges can be calculated reliably. For a fixed offer, Elecz estimates the annual cost from its price terms. For a PUN-indexed offer, the cost varies with the national wholesale index. Elecz uses the offer’s charges and its sensitivity to the index to estimate a cost range under historical PUN scenarios.

Historical PUN and the break-even price

Elecz uses a recent window of 12 consecutive completed months from the monthly Portale Offerte Open Data series. The lowest and highest monthly values in that window are used as scenarios. They give a sense of how costs vary with the index. The break-even PUN, when shown, is the index price at which the fixed and indexed offers shown have the same estimated annual electricity sales cost.

If the latest completed month is missing from the official series, Elecz can use an estimate derived from ENTSO-E A44 day-ahead zonal prices weighted by consumption. This applies only to that month and requires a validated estimate and the preceding 11 official monthly values. When the official monthly value is included in the reference data, it takes precedence over the estimate. Some offers priced by time band also require verified ratios for the same historical window.

Coverage and eligibility

Coverage counts the household offers compared out of those in the source snapshot. The comparison excludes confirmed restricted offers, such as those reserved for specific groups or available only with another product. For some offers, Elecz cannot establish eligibility from the available information, for example when conditions depend on residency or are ambiguous. Elecz leaves those offers out of the results.

Elecz interprets two structured ARERA fields, the supplier-change code and the condition restriction flag, using secondary descriptions of the data format. The official AU specification was unavailable for verification.

The web comparison does not collect your region. Region-limited or more local offers may therefore be missing. When excluded offers might be cheaper and the calculation reports partial coverage, Elecz can show offers it was able to price without recommending one. Unsupported tariff structures and missing price data can also limit coverage.
