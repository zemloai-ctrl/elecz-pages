Use your own numbers

Bring your four-digit postcode and annual use in kWh from your electricity bills. Add a confirmed meter type if you know it.

Compare what applies

If several distributor options apply to a postcode, the service may compare only plans available across every listed option.

Understand the estimate

Annual estimates are in AUD and include GST. They are not guaranteed bills or a promise of the cheapest plan in the whole market.

Choosing your exact distributor can reveal additional or cheaper plans. If a safe shared comparison is not possible, the service will ask you to confirm the right option with your retailer.

New South Wales first

This preview is for NSW households changing electricity retailer. Other states and territories: Coming later.

Only plans supported by the current comparison rules can be included. Time-of-use or demand pricing that needs a detailed usage profile may not be comparable from annual use alone.

Look beyond one number

The annual estimate helps you compare the returned plans. Before choosing, check the retailer’s usage rate, daily supply charge, eligibility and full terms. Elecz does not invent missing rates, discounts or savings.

Plan data: Australian Energy Regulator / Energy Made Easy. Availability and comparison coverage are explained with each result when the service is available.
