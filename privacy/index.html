Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

Elecz is operated by Sakari Korkia-Aho (SKA Trading Oy / Zemlo AI), Finland.
Contact: sakke@elecz.com

What data we collect

When you use the Elecz API, website, or MCP server, we may process limited technical information required to operate and secure the service, including:

  • Requested endpoint and query parameters (for example: zone=DE)
  • Timestamp of the request
  • Truncated IP address information used for security and abuse prevention
  • User-agent information
  • Error and diagnostic information related to service operation

What we do not collect

Elecz is designed to minimize data collection. We do not intentionally collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or account credentials
  • Payment information
  • Chat messages or conversation history
  • User profiles or advertising identifiers

Elecz does not require user accounts, login credentials, API keys, or authentication for normal use.

How we use data

Technical data is processed solely to operate the service, monitor availability and performance, detect abuse and security incidents, debug errors, and produce aggregate usage statistics. Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data retention

  • Request logs: retained for up to 90 days, then automatically deleted
  • Aggregated usage statistics and counters: retained indefinitely
  • Redis cache: TTL-based, maximum 24 hours
  • Cloudflare edge logs: per Cloudflare's applicable retention policies

Third-party processors

Elecz uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate the service. These providers may process technical request data on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — network delivery, security, DDoS protection
  • Supabase — database hosting (EU region)
  • Upstash — Redis caching

These providers act as data processors and process data only as necessary to provide infrastructure services.

Data sources

Electricity market data displayed by Elecz originates from public or licensed market sources including ENTSO-E, Octopus Energy Agile API, AEMO, EM6, CAISO OASIS, ERCOT CDR, NYISO MIS, IESO, KPX EPSIS, and JEPX via japanesepower.org.

MCP server and AI assistants

The MCP endpoint at https://elecz.com/mcp may receive requests from AI assistants and agent platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, and other MCP-compatible clients. Elecz receives only the parameters necessary to fulfill the request, such as zone identifiers, consumption estimates, and tool parameters. Elecz does not receive account information, names, email addresses, or conversation history unless such information is explicitly transmitted by the client. Elecz does not attempt to identify individual users.

International users

Elecz is operated from Finland. Depending on the infrastructure provider used, limited technical request data may be processed in the European Union or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. Appropriate contractual and technical safeguards are used where applicable.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect service data against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No system can guarantee absolute security, but Elecz follows industry-standard practices appropriate for a small-scale infrastructure service.

User rights and controls

You may request information regarding data associated with your use of Elecz, or request deletion of such data, by contacting sakke@elecz.com. We will respond within a reasonable period and, where required by law, within applicable legal deadlines.

GDPR (EU users)

If you are located in the European Union, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability where applicable.

The legal basis for processing technical request logs is our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and maintaining the service under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. To exercise your rights, contact sakke@elecz.com.

Third-party links

Elecz may contain links to third-party websites including electricity suppliers and energy-related services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.
